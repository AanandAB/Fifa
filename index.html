About the Tournament

Everything you need to know about FIFA World Cup 2026

📋 Format

  • 48 teams · 12 groups of 4 · 104 total matches
  • Group stage: June 11–27 — each team plays 3 matches
  • Top 2 from each group + 8 best 3rd-place teams → 32 advance
  • Round of 32 → Round of 16 → Quarterfinals → Semifinals → Final
  • Two separate bracket pathways keep Spain & Argentina in opposite halves

🏟️ Host Nations

  • 🇺🇸 United States — 11 venues across the country
  • 🇨🇦 Canada — 2 venues: Toronto & Vancouver
  • 🇲🇽 Mexico — 3 venues: Mexico City, Zapopan, Monterrey
  • Final: MetLife Stadium, East Rutherford, New Jersey · July 19
  • Opening match: Mexico vs South Africa · Estadio Azteca · June 11

🌟 Tournament Facts

  • First 48-team World Cup in history
  • 4 debutants: Cape Verde, Curaçao, Jordan, Uzbekistan
  • Defending champions: 🇦🇷 Argentina (2022 winners)
  • Most titles: 🇧🇷 Brazil (5)
  • Hosted across 16 stadiums in 3 countries

🏆 Trophy

The FIFA World Cup Trophy stands 36.8 cm tall, weighs 6.175 kg, and is made of 18-carat gold. It will be awarded to the 2026 champions at MetLife Stadium on July 19.
